def find_max_average(nums, k):
    window_sum = sum(nums[:k])
    best = window_sum
    for right in range(k, len(nums)):
        window_sum += nums[right] - nums[right-k]
        best = max(best, window_sum)
    return best / k

Your first launch

Open Cue.
Step by step.

This developer preview is locally signed and isn’t notarized by Apple. Use the normal macOS approval flow for Cue individually.

  1. Download the ZIP for your Mac’s chip. Move cue.app into Applications.
  2. Open Cue. If macOS shows an unverified-developer warning, choose Done (or Cancel / OK).
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway for Cue.
  4. Confirm Open. Add your own provider key in Cue Settings, then grant capture access when you use it.

Before you download.

Is Cue free?

Yes. Cue is free and open source under GPL-3.0-or-later. There is no required Cue account or subscription. You bring your own provider key, and the provider may charge for AI or cloud transcription usage.

Which Macs does it work on?

The complete meeting-audio experience requires macOS 14.4 or later. Choose the separate Apple silicon or Intel download that matches your Mac. A local Whisper model must be downloaded separately to use local transcription.

Why does macOS say “Cue” Not Opened?

This developer preview is signed with a local development certificate. It is not signed with an Apple Developer ID and is not Apple-notarized. Use the first-launch guide to approve Cue through Privacy & Security if you trust the download.

Will Cue appear in my screen share?

Incognito requests native screen-capture protection, and starts enabled on each fresh launch. Capture behavior varies: some ScreenCaptureKit-based tools can include protected windows. Test your exact screen-share setup. It does not hide macOS recording indicators.

Does it hear both sides of a call?

With the corresponding permissions, Cue transcribes your microphone and meeting audio as separate You and Them channels. Meeting audio is opt-in under Settings → Audio. Choose Local Whisper or a supported cloud speech provider.

Where does my data go?

Settings, keys, modes, reference files, and saved sessions stay in local application storage. AI requests send the needed context to your selected provider. Cloud speech sends audio to the chosen speech provider; Local Whisper processes speech on your Mac. Read the privacy details.

Can I use my own models and meeting context?

Yes. Choose Fast and Smart models, save custom IDs, or use a compatible endpoint. Meeting modes have editable titles, instructions, and reference files, with automatic saving and your own preferred order.
