Your first launch

A little help.
A simple setup.

Choose the download for your Mac, then follow the normal macOS first-launch steps.

Choose your Mac.

The files below are clean source builds, signed with a local development certificate. They are not signed with an Apple Developer ID and are not Apple-notarized. macOS may block the first launch.

Install and open Cue.

  1. Download the ZIP that matches your Mac. Unzip it and move cue.app into Applications. Keep one installed copy.
  2. Open Cue from Applications. If macOS shows the unverified-developer warning, choose Done, Cancel, or OK.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security. Scroll to Security and choose Open Anyway for Cue.
  4. Confirm Open and authenticate on your Mac if it asks. Keep Gatekeeper enabled.

For a “damaged” or “will damage your computer” warning, stop and report the issue. Download only from this website or its linked Cue GitHub release.

Add your model, then your permissions.

Open Settings → API Keys, choose your provider, and add your own key. Provider usage is billed by that provider. You can save separate Fast and Smart model choices.

Microphone access is needed for your speech. Screen Recording is needed for screen context and opt-in meeting audio. Start capture when you want to use it; this website never requests microphone or screen access.

For local speech, choose Settings → Audio → Local and download a Whisper model. The native runtime is included in the preview; model files download separately.

Check your download.

Compare the SHA-256 shown above with the file you downloaded. The release manifest also contains exact file sizes, architecture, signing status, and the source revision.

Updates are manual. Quit Cue, replace the app in Applications, and keep a single installed copy. Saved settings, modes, chat threads, preferences, and recordings are stored separately from the app.
