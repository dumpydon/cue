A little help.
On your Mac.

A clear setup, an honest preview, and your choice of models.

Developer preview. This build is locally certificate-signed. It is not signed with an Apple Developer ID and is not Apple-notarized. macOS may block the first launch. Download only if you are comfortable evaluating a developer preview.

Choose your Mac

For the complete meeting-audio experience, use macOS 14.4 or later. Open Apple menu → About This Mac to check your processor and macOS version.

Checking preview downloads… You can also download the complete source.

Install without a Terminal

  1. Download the preview matching your Mac. Open the ZIP and move cue.app into your Applications folder.
  2. Open Cue from Applications. If macOS blocks an unrecognized developer, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, review the message for Cue, and choose Open Anyway only if you trust this preview. You may need to try opening Cue first for that option to appear.
  3. Confirm macOS’s prompt. Managed Macs may not allow this; follow your organization’s policy. Do not turn off Gatekeeper or your Mac’s general security protections.
  4. Add your chosen provider key in Cue’s Settings. Start a session when you are ready; grant microphone and screen or system-audio access only for features you want to use.

Apple explains the normal first-launch controls in Open a Mac app from an unknown developer.

First session

Cue opens in incognito. The gray indicator beneath the incognito button becomes red when you switch to visible mode. Choose a meeting mode, add context if useful, and select Fast or Smart. Microphone and meeting audio are separate inputs; meeting audio is opt-in on macOS.

Local transcription needs a downloaded Whisper model. The model itself is not bundled because of its size. Cloud providers may charge for API usage.
