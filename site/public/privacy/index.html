Privacy,
in plain language.

Cue runs on your desktop. The providers you choose determine where AI requests are processed.

On this website

This website does not access your microphone, capture your screen, or run the Cue desktop app. Product demonstrations contain synthetic example content. There are no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or tracking cookies. Cloudflare serves the website and may process normal request metadata to deliver and protect it. Download links may lead to GitHub’s release hosting.

What stays on your Mac

The app keeps its settings, provider credentials, saved model choices, meeting modes, imported reference files, and saved sessions in local application storage. Transcript turns can be saved locally during a session so history and recaps remain available. Local storage is not a promise of encryption at rest.

What goes to an AI provider

When you ask for help, Cue sends relevant prompt context to the provider or compatible endpoint you select. Depending on the action and your settings, that can include your question, recent transcript, meeting-mode instructions, reference-file text, profile context, or a screenshot. The selected provider’s terms, retention policies, and pricing apply.

Audio and local transcription

Cloud transcription sends audio to the selected speech provider. Local Whisper transcription processes audio on your Mac after the runtime and a model are installed. Local transcription does not automatically make cloud chat local: a cloud chat provider still receives the request context needed to answer.

Capture and incognito are different controls

Microphone and screen or system-audio capture require macOS permission. Meeting audio is opt-in on macOS. The system may show a recording or sharing indicator while capture is active.

Incognito requests native protection against screen capture and starts enabled on fresh launches. It does not hide macOS recording indicators, and it cannot guarantee exclusion from every capture tool. Some ScreenCaptureKit-based apps can include protected windows. Test your actual setup before relying on it.

Your responsibility and control

Use capture in accordance with the consent requirements and rules that apply to your meeting. You can stop a session, change providers, control meeting audio, and choose which context to include. This guide describes the current developer preview, not a separate cloud account or hosted AI service.

Questions or concerns

Open a GitHub issue for questions about the implementation. Do not include passwords, API keys, personal transcripts, or sensitive screenshots in a public report.
