0.4.14 · Developer preview

The Cue Mac preview.

A desktop AI copilot for conversations, questions, and code, with your own provider credentials.

  • Screen context, microphone speech, and opt-in meeting audio in separate channels.
  • Saved sessions, transcript history, replies, and recaps.
  • Editable meeting modes with saved titles, instructions, reference files, and custom order.
  • Syntax-highlighted code, formatted mathematics, copy controls, code wrapping, and reading controls.
  • Incognito at fresh launch, a dotted border, and a red visible-mode indicator.
  • Apple silicon and Intel ZIPs with an included local Whisper runtime; speech models download separately.

This is a local-certificate-signed developer preview, not an Apple Developer ID or notarized release. Read the installation guide.
